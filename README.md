## Favorite Existing Apps - Categorize and Evaluate
### Instagram
   - **Category:** Photo & Video / Social 
   - **Mobile:** Website is view only, uses camera, mobile first experience.
   - **Story:** Allows users to share their lives in pictures and enhance their content with filters
   - **Market:** Anyone that takes pictures could enjoy this app. Ability to follow and hashtag based on interests and categories allows users with unique interests to engage with relevant content.
   - **Habit:** Users can post throughout the day many times. Features like "Stories" encourage more candid posting as well. Users can explore endless pictures in any category imaginable whenever they want. Very habbit forming!
   - **Scope:** Instagram started out extremely narrow focused, just posting pics and viewing feeds. Has expanded to a somewhat larger scope including "Instagram Stories" (a la SnapChat) and messenger features.
### X (formerly Twitter)
  - **Category:** Social Media platform
  - **Mobile:** Website uses camera, location, can use maps. Mobile and desktop friendly
  - **Story:** Gives users a limited (unless one pays a premium) space in order to post a story in either a couple of words and/or pictures. Neither is preferred but both can be used.
  - **Market:** Anyone could enjoy this app, taking pictures or having something to say in writing is not a necesity to use and browse the app
  - **Habit:** There is no known limit of how much a user can post on their X account, stories can range from current events to tech news to spaces for social interraction via Direct Messaging(DM)
  - **Scope:** X, or known during its inception as Twitter, started out as a social media platform which gave users a place to share thoughts through short stories in word format. Eventually it would grow to allow photos, videos(live or recorded) and even Spaces which allows for real-time chats with other users in somehting like a chat room.
### YouTube
  - **Category:** Entertainment / Social
  - **Mobile:** Website is mobile and desktop friendly, users can use built in camera to record live video or upload and edit prerecorded videos even in short format known as "Shorts"
  - **Story:** Users can upload videos or Shorts about their lives or any topic they wish so as long as it is within certain guidelines
  - **Market:** Users rnaging from various age groups, the site has a rigorous algorithm that learns a user's preferences based on their search and watch history
  - **Habit:** As mentioned above, the YouTube algorithm recommends videos to users based on their preferences creating a sort of "endless scroll" across these preferences.
  - **Scope:** Started as a humble video hosting site, when acquired by Google their adsense algorithm became more and more persistent on suggesting specific ads to users based on the preferences gathered by the previously mentioned algorithm.
### Google Maps
  - **Category:** Travel
  - **Mobile:** Mostly used as a mobile app for users on the go looking to get to some unknown place or for the sake of travel updates on their route
  - **Story:** Used mostly for convenience, as a reliable way to use the phones GPS and Network to get to a particular place
  - **Market:** Mostly for anyone who drives or commutes on a regular basis, not so  much the commuters but still useful for them if their usual way to work (bus, train or even ferry) is down
  - **Habit:** Since Google Maps is the map of the world, users can search up any place on earth even using coordinates (latitude, longitude) to see a place in the world via a bird's eye view , even 3D positioning is used in some parts giving users a feel of what the location they are looking at is like.
  - **Scope:** Google Maps was started in February of 2005 as a desktop application written in C++.
### Notes
  - **Category:** Productivity
  - **Mobile:** Not as much a website, since it is Apple's built-in note taking app which comes stock in every device they sell. Users can sync across their Apple devices using iCloud for seamless integration across them
  - **Story:** Used to take and keep notes. Users can draw, insert photos or videos, include links and even other files like PDFs.
  - **Market:** Useful for anyone in the technical field where note taking might be essential in their workflows, even college students can benefit from having a notepad built into their phones
  - **Habit:** Can be addictive but mostly used for note taking, great place to put down ideas when brainstorming like this project! 
  - **Scope:** Started as a simple note taking app, over time Apple developers evolved it into something almost like a word processor as users can export anything in the app into PDF or DOC format for more formal use.
### Messages
  - **Category:** Social
  - **Mobile:** Mainly used on mobile platforms, Apple recently incorporated the use of Messages on their tablets and Desktops for seamless integration via iCloud.
  - **Story:** Very convenient for Apple users and their devices as users will be able to see real-time reactions, replies and even play games together all within the app. Creating a sort of social circle amongst iPhone                  users
  - **Market:** Since Apple's products are very popular, especially with the younger demographic, this app the user base is very large.
  - **Habit:** Though not a social networking app itself, Messages encourages iPhone users to come together and utilize these exclusive features that other mobile platforms may not, thus creating it's own sort of social                  network based on that fact. The ease of use amongst that exclusive user base creates that
  - **Scope:** Messages started out same as any other stock messaging app, over time it evolved to become an all-in-one Messaging app even allowing file sharing on the platform amongst users thus bringing social circles                  together
### Facebook Messanger
   - **Category:** Social
   - **Mobile:** Mainly only used on mobile platforms, Facebook (by Meta) has is integrated with the website on Desktop where users can communicate, mobile platforms ONLY do this through the Facebook Messenger app
   - **Story:** Provides a means of communication between Facebook users where phone and video calls can be had with any user and live chatting regardless of platform unlike Apple's aforementioned Messages app
   - **Market:** Facebook requires it's users to be 18 years or older to access its platform. Mainly used to keep in touch with friends and family
   - **Habit:** Can be very addicting as many users use the app as a primary form of communication, especially when no cell service is feasible, Facebook Messenger uses internet to bridge its users.
   - **Scope:** Facebook Messenger became an instant messaging app in March 2008 starting from desktop to mobile, around when Facebook rose to popularity. Now integrates with Instagram for seamless messaging across the                    Meta platform
### Pokemon TCG Pocket
   - **Category:** Entertainment
   - **Mobile:** Strictly only on mobile platforms unless emulated, touch gestures are deeply integrated into this app
   - **Story:** This card game gives the user the experience of opening up a pack of cards, building decks for play and archiving collections all from their device
   - **Market:** The game is accomodating to every sort of fan of the Pokemon franchise, new and old, that all age groups can actually enjoy
   - **Habit:** As of the writing of this document, the Pokemon craze has had a resurgence with fans seeking cards from major retailers to the point that a casual fan can't get ahold of any cards. This app creates that                    experience for the user, without the need to even spend money! Unless the user chooses to of course
   - **Scope:** Still a fairly new app, but its popularity is worldwide as the Pokemon craze has resurfaced due to the pandemic seeing no downward trends years later.
### Clock
   - **Category:** Productivity
   - **Mobile:** Only available on Apple's iPhone and iPad, syncs across Apple devices through iCloud
   - **Story:** Used for seein world clocks and setting timers or alarms, essential for any working adult and/or college student!
   - **Market:** Everyone needs a reliable clock, same for alarms
   - **Habit:** None really, the app is very basic but useful
   - **Scope:** A stock clock app. Useful for any and all situations.
